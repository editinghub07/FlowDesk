The new version is designed for automatic Google Drive authorization renewal and faster cross-device sync.

Before switching

  1. Open your old app, sync your Google workspace and export a backup of all records, including Guest records.
  2. Open the new app and sign in with the same Google account. Verify that your Drive records are present.
  3. Install the new app from its new address after testing. Keep the old app until your data is confirmed.
