Privacy & data

FlowDesk is a personal workspace for tracking habits, expenses and editing projects. Google Drive synchronization is optional.

Data FlowDesk uses

In guest mode, your records stay in your browser. When you connect Google, FlowDesk requests your Google account identifier, email address and profile name, and permission to access its own Google Drive app-data folder. It uses your identity to separate local profiles and associate your snapshots with your account. It does not request access to your other Drive files.

Storage and synchronization

When connected, FlowDesk uploads copies of your recorded habits, expenses, manual wallet balances and transfers, clients, projects and income records to the private app-data folder of your own Google Drive. Older snapshots are retained for recovery. A local copy is saved in browser storage so the app can work offline. This release does not transmit your tracker data to a FlowDesk-operated server or include advertising or analytics code. The hosting provider and Google may process technical information in accordance with their own policies.

Access and security

Google access tokens are kept in memory, not saved to local storage. Local records are not separately encrypted or protected by a password. Anyone who has access to your browser profile may be able to access them. Do not use a shared device for confidential records. Browser storage may be erased by the operating system or browser. Export backups regularly.

Your choices and deletion

Sign out to return to guest mode. Use Remove local profile to delete that Google account's cached records on this device. This does not delete Drive copies. To remove hidden FlowDesk app data, open Google Drive settings, Manage apps, find FlowDesk and use its hidden-data deletion option if available. You can revoke authorization in your Google Account connections. Revoking authorization does not necessarily delete existing records. You may also clear browser site data to delete remaining guest records and settings. Exported backups must be deleted separately.

Google API data

FlowDesk's use of information received from Google APIs is limited to providing account identification, backup, restore and synchronization features requested by the user, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Contact

For questions or requests, contact the maintainer through the support or Issues page of the GitHub repository hosting this app. The publisher should provide a dedicated support email before public release.
